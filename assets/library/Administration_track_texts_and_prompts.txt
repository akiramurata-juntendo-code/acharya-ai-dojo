TRAINING GROUNDS - drills with a text and a prompt

--- From meeting notes to an action list
NOTES, office meeting, 3 November 2027 (fictional). Orientation leaflets: the print office will deliver 500 copies by 10 November. Room for the workshop: still not booked - someone should ask Facilities. Website notice: the Training Office will draft it; no date agreed. Feedback form from last term: discuss next time.

“From the notes above, make a numbered action list: action | owner | deadline. Use only what the notes say. If an owner or a deadline is missing, write NOT STATED. Do not add actions that the notes do not mention.”

--- Your own best documents: same style, new facts
PAST NOTICE (already public, fictional): Library closed on 18 September. The Central Library will be closed on Friday, 18 September 2026, for stock-taking. Books due on that day may be returned on Monday, 21 September, without a fine. Library Office

NEW FACTS (fictional): computer lab 3 closed wednesday 14 oct 2026 for software updates. use lab 1 instead. IT Office

“Above are a past public notice and new facts. Write the new notice in the same style and length as the past notice. Use only the new facts; do not change any date, room or name (you may write them out in full). If a detail is missing, write [TO BE CONFIRMED]; do not invent it.”

--- A voice note becomes a task list
TRANSCRIPT (fictional): ok so tomorrow, um, first I need to call the print office about the leaflets, then the budget sheet, that has to go to the manager by Thursday noon, and oh, I should book the projector for Friday's briefing, and maybe at some point tidy the shared folder

“Above is a transcript of me thinking aloud. Turn it into a numbered task list. Mark anything that sounds like a deadline. Do not add tasks I did not mention.”
