How the belts work

    You tick the boxes yourself when you have done a mission or a drill. The belts are a study guide, not a certificate, and nothing leaves your phone.

    Your three missionsPen first, phone second. Tap a mission. “Copy prompt + text” gives you both in one piece: paste it into the assistant you normally use, and send.

    Training groundsShort drills for after the session. All example material is fictional. Progress is kept only on this device.
